Privacy Policy — Irrigation Troubleshooter

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app has no internet access. It does not request the network permission and cannot send data anywhere. All content is stored on the device as part of the app.

The app saves your position in the troubleshooting guide, whether you have accepted the disclaimer, and your “keep screen on” preference on your own device so it can resume where you left off. This is stored locally, is excluded from Android backups, and is removed when the app is uninstalled. It is never transmitted.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party services of any kind.

If you share a troubleshooting result using your device’s share feature, that text goes wherever you choose to send it. The app is not involved beyond handing the text to the app you pick.
